Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Weird Seal Games ("we", "us") operates the Pack It All mobile game ("the Game").

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information. However, third-party services used in the Game may collect data:

  • Google AdMob: Collects device information and advertising identifiers to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GameAnalytics: Collects gameplay data for analytics purposes. See GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

The Game stores game progress and settings locally on your device using PlayerPrefs. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

The Game displays ads provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
